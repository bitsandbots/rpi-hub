GPS Sky Survey

A diagnostic sweep of all 32 GPS satellites at 1575.42 MHz: which birds are visible, their Doppler offset, and relative signal strength. Requires the RTL-SDR with an active GPS patch antenna (bias tee on) and clear sky view.

What this is not: a position or time fix. The sweep stops at acquisition — it proves the antenna chain works and shows sky state. Receive-only, like everything else on this hub.

No RTL-SDR dongle detected. Plug one in and refresh; nothing else on the hub depends on the radio.
